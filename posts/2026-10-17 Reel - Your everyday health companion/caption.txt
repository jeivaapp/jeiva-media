Most of us only think about our health when something feels wrong. Then it's a rush of appointments, reports we don't fully understand, and advice that changes depending on who we ask.

We think it should feel different.

Jeiva is a daily health companion built for India. It brings the small signals your body gives you every day together with the bigger picture from your check-ups and lab reports, and explains what they mean in plain language. No jargon. No panic. Just a clearer view of how you're doing, and a gentle nudge when something is worth a conversation with your doctor.

We're starting with Cycle Intelligence, because we're putting her first. Women's health is so often the health that gets postponed: put after work, after family, after everyone else. Your cycle is one of the clearest signals your body gives, and Jeiva is being built to read it alongside everything else.

And when something needs a closer look, you shouldn't have to search for the right doctor. We're building a panel of specialists across gynaecology, reproductive medicine, gut and liver health, lungs, children's surgery and mental health, so your check-ups are with people who know their field.

We're opening early access to a small group first, so we can build it with the people who'll use it. Early members will help shape what Jeiva becomes.
